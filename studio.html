Our story

Maison Noir began in a Paris apartment in 2012 with a single belief: that design, at its best, is quiet. Today we're a team of twelve across four cities, still driven by the same conviction, and still small enough to care about every pixel.

What we believe

Four convictions.

Precision over volume

We take on a handful of projects each year so every one gets the attention it deserves. No factory, no shortcuts.

Collaboration, not hierarchy

Our clients are partners in the process. The best ideas win, regardless of where they come from in the room.

Built to endure

We design for the decade, not the quarter. Our work is meant to age gracefully, not chase the zeitgeist.

Restraint as a virtue

We remove more than we add. The discipline to know what to leave out is what makes a brand feel inevitable.
